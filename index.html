Where your complaint goes

Five bureaus and cells handle every case.

  • Anti-Ragging Cellरैगिंग-रोधी प्रकोष्ठRagging, harassment, threats, safety
  • Finance Bureauवित्त ब्यूरोFees, refunds, scholarships
  • Approval Bureauअनुमोदन ब्यूरोApproval, affiliation, fake courses
  • Academic Bureauशैक्षणिक ब्यूरोFaculty, syllabus, exams, placements
  • Grievance Redressal Cellशिकायत निवारण प्रकोष्ठAnything else

Keep these ready

  • Full name and institution, with its city
  • What happened, and roughly when
  • Any earlier reply from the college

Who is signing in?

Students file complaints and follow their own cases. Officials monitor and update every case.

How it works

  1. 1

    Sign inसाइन इन करें

    Choose student or AICTE official and continue.

  2. 2

    Describe the problemसमस्या बताएँ

    Write in your own words. No forms to choose from.

  3. 3

    It reaches the right cellसही प्रकोष्ठ तक पहुँचती है

    The complaint is routed and given an urgency automatically.

  4. 4

    The college is toldसंस्थान को सूचित किया जाता है

    An official reviews it and asks the college to reply within 7 days.

  5. 5

    Track until it is resolvedनिपटारे तक प्रगति देखें

    Follow New, In review, Sent to college and Resolved.
